## Abstract

The first massive black holes formed as light or heavy seeds and grew into the giants we see today. Electromagnetic (EM) surveys measure their growth by accretion, but they cannot see the small black holes at early times. LISA will detect mergers of these small black holes out to z ≈ 20. We build one population model that describes both data sets. It follows the black-hole mass function from z ≈ 20 to z = 0, with accretion, mergers, light and heavy seeds, and a delay between galaxy and black-hole mergers. We fit EM data (AGN luminosity functions, the local black-hole mass function and JWST AGN) together with mock LISA detections.
